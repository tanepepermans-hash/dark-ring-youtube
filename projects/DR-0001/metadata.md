## Description
Sonny Liston, one of the most feared heavyweight champions in boxing history, was
found dead in his Las Vegas home in January 1971. The coroner ruled it natural causes.
Almost no one close to him believed that was the full story. This is a short,
source-based look at what was — and wasn't — found at the scene.
